❓ How to Play ClueRush Live

👥 2–8 Players & Fair Turn Rotation

Every round, one player is rotated in as the Hint Giver. They pick a secret answer within 25 seconds, and everyone races a synchronized 120-second countdown to guess it!

Mode A: Numbers

Pick a secret number (1–100 or custom), select digit count, and send live clues: Even/Odd, Greater/Less Than, or Ranges.

Mode B: Word Draw

Pick a category & secret word, then draw it live! No writing letters. Optional 1st-letter hint costs -15 pts.

Mode C: Draw & Guess

Choose Easy (+0), Medium (+15 bonus), or Hard (+30 bonus) word and draw it while friends race to guess!
